About five minutes, once

Put BOWELS on your watch.

Log a session from your wrist while your phone stays in the other room. Your watch reads your heart rate the whole time and hands everything to your phone when they're back together.

⌚A Wear OS 3+ watch (Galaxy Watch 4 or newer, Pixel Watch, and others)
📱BOWELS on your phone, paired with that watch
💻A computer on the same Wi-Fi as the watch
0 of 6 done
The watch app arrives with BOWELS 3.0, which isn't published yet. You can read through the steps now; the watch app button starts working the moment the release is out.

If something's off

Quick fixes

Most hiccups come from the Wi-Fi or a changed port. These cover nearly all of them.

"Failed to connect" or the pair command hangs

Check that the computer and the watch are on the same Wi-Fi (not a guest network), and that Wireless debugging is still on. The ports change every time Wireless debugging restarts, so read the address on the watch again.

"adb" isn't recognized

The terminal isn't in the platform-tools folder. Open the folder first, then the terminal from inside it. On Windows the commands start with .\adb, on Mac and Linux with ./adb; the switch in step 4 sets that for you.

INSTALL_FAILED_UPDATE_INCOMPATIBLE

An older BOWELS build signed differently is already on the watch. Uninstall it from the watch (long-press the app › Uninstall) and run the install command again.

The watch logs sessions but nothing shows up on the phone

The phone and watch apps must come from the same release, so download both from the latest one. Also make sure the watch is paired with that phone in the Wear OS or Galaxy Wearable app. Sessions wait safely on the watch until the two can talk.

No heart rate on the watch

BOWELS asks for heart-rate access the first time you start a session. If you said no, open Settings › Apps › BOWELS › Permissions on the watch and allow it. The timer works either way.

You're set

This is what's on your wrist now.

Start a session, watch your heart rate as you go, and answer a few taps at the end. Your phone gets everything the next time they're together.
